## Final generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial triptych of sample vertical video/Reel covers for a premium video-saving website. Create one landscape image, 1536 by 1024. Exactly three equally wide, perfectly edge-to-edge vertical photographic panels, no borders or gaps. Left panel: a sunlit winding coastal road along rocky Mediterranean cliffs and a deep teal sea, shot from above, warm late-afternoon light. Middle panel: close-up of hands pouring milk into espresso in a white ceramic cup, warm cafe window light and rich tactile details, composition focused on the cup and hands only. Right panel: a small lone hiker in an ochre jacket seen from behind on a mountain ridge under soft clouds, cinematic natural landscape with dusty green and blue mountains. The panels should feel like thoughtful stills from high-quality travel, coffee, and outdoor short videos, authentic photography, natural grain, consistent refined muted color grading, beautiful but not overly saturated. The content fills the whole canvas with no external background. No text, numbers, captions, UI, phone frames, play buttons, logos, watermarks, gradients, or graphic decoration. Each panel is a distinct photo and its subject stays in the middle of its own third.
