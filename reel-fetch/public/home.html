What is Reel Fetch?+

Reel Fetch is a tool for previewing and downloading eligible public Instagram videos and Reels. Paste a supported link, choose an available format, and save the file.

Is Reel Fetch free?+

This version is free to use. There are no subscriptions, advertisements, or paid download tiers.

Do I need an account?+

No. Reel Fetch does not ask you to sign up or provide an Instagram username, password, or session cookie.

Can I download private Instagram content?+

No. Reel Fetch does not access private accounts or bypass login requirements, access controls, or DRM.

Can Reel Fetch download every Reel?+

No. Deleted posts, restricted content, login requirements, platform blocks, and rate limits can prevent access. A public link does not guarantee that a download is permitted or technically available. This version supports videos up to 20 minutes and files up to 250 MB. Carousel posts use the first video.

Where are downloaded files saved?+

Your browser saves the file to its usual download location, often your Downloads folder. On mobile, check your browser’s downloads or Files app. Your browser settings may ask you to choose a folder.

Does Reel Fetch store downloaded videos?+

Processing uses temporary files. Source files are reused temporarily and expire 30 minutes after the latest processing attempt. Finished files expire 30 minutes after preparation. Cleanup runs every minute while the server is running; interrupted files are cleaned up when it restarts. Reel Fetch does not create a public media archive. See our Privacy Policy.

Is Reel Fetch affiliated with Instagram or Meta?+

No. Reel Fetch is an independent service. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.

What content am I allowed to download?+

Only content you own, have the necessary permission to download, or are otherwise legally entitled to save through the supported method. Check rights for the video and any music or other included material, and follow applicable platform rules. Public availability alone is not permission. You are responsible for your use of the content.

What quality and audio can I download?+

Best available keeps the source quality. 1080p, 720p, and 360p appear only when the source dimensions support them; we do not upscale. You can export available audio as MP3. Reel Fetch adds no watermark and does not remove marks already in the video.
